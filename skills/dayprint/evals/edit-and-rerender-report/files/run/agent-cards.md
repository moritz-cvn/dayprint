# Agent cards

One agent per job found in your work. Each works in any agent tool: ChatGPT or Claude projects, custom GPTs, n8n, Zapier, or Cavion.

## Proposal writer

Turns discovery-call notes into a proposal in the studio's structure.

- **Starts:** on request, After a discovery call
- **Reads:** Call notes
- **Tools:** Documents (write)
- **Needs:** Proposal structure, Services and prices
- **Delivers:** A Word proposal
- **You approve:** A person reviews and sends the proposal.
- **Saves:** 0.8 to 1.1 hours a week

```text
Turn the call notes into a proposal with five parts: goals, scope, timeline, price, next steps. Price from the price list: day rate, split into design and build, and always offer a maintenance retainer. German unless asked; add an English scope section when asked. If budget, deadline or page count is missing, ask one question first. Deliver a document for review.
```

## Status writer

Drafts the weekly client status update from the week's notes and tasks.

- **Starts:** schedule, Fridays at 14:00
- **Reads:** Tasks of the week, Call notes, Team chat threads
- **Tools:** Notion (read), Slack (read), Gmail (draft)
- **Needs:** Status template, Brand voice
- **Delivers:** One draft per client
- **You approve:** A person reads and sends each mail.
- **Saves:** 0.5 to 0.7 hours a week

```text
Every Friday, for each active client project, read this week's tasks, call notes and team chat threads. Write one status update per client in German, friendly and at most 120 words, in three parts: done, next, what we need from you. Follow the brand voice and the status template. Add the staging link when there is one. Deliver the drafts for review; never send them.
```

## Invoice preparer

Prepares monthly invoice lines and overdue reminders.

- **Starts:** schedule, First working day of the month, 9:00
- **Reads:** Time-tracking export, Open invoices
- **Tools:** Time tracking (Toggl Track) (read), Billing mailbox (draft)
- **Needs:** Invoicing process, Services and prices
- **Delivers:** Invoice lines and reminder drafts
- **You approve:** A person books the invoices and sends the reminders.
- **Saves:** 0.3 to 0.5 hours a week

```text
Read last month's tracked hours and group them per client project. Make one invoice line per project at the day rate from the price list and flag entries without a project. Then list invoices unpaid for more than 14 days and draft a polite German reminder for each. Deliver the lines as a table and the reminders as drafts. Never book invoices, send mails or move money.
```

## Call-notes tasker

Turns client call notes into tasks with owners and due dates.

- **Starts:** new item, New call notes are added
- **Reads:** Call notes
- **Tools:** Notion (write)
- **Needs:** Team roles
- **Delivers:** Tasks with owners
- **You approve:** None needed; tasks stay internal.
- **Saves:** 0.3 to 0.5 hours a week

```text
For each new call note, list the action items. Give each an owner (founder, designer or developer) and a due date; use the next call date when none is said. Create the tasks on the task board and post the list back to the project.
```

## Post drafter

Drafts LinkedIn posts about recent launches.

- **Starts:** schedule, Fridays at 15:30
- **Reads:** Recent launches, Case studies
- **Tools:** Notion (read)
- **Needs:** Brand voice, Post ideas
- **Delivers:** Two drafts, three hooks each
- **You approve:** A person picks one and posts it.
- **Saves:** 0.3 to 0.5 hours a week

```text
Look at launches and case studies from the past two weeks. Draft two LinkedIn posts of at most 120 words: the client's problem, what was built, one result. Give three alternative opening hooks for each. Use the brand voice; no hashtag lists. Deliver the drafts; never post.
```

## Week planner

Prepares a short brief for Monday planning.

- **Starts:** schedule, Mondays at 8:30
- **Reads:** Calendar, Mail
- **Tools:** Calendar (read), Mail (read)
- **Delivers:** A short brief
- **You approve:** None needed; read only.
- **Saves:** 0.2 to 0.3 hours a week

```text
Read this week's calendar and last week's unanswered mail. Write a brief under 200 words: calls that need preparation with one line of context each, deadlines, threads waiting on a reply, and three suggested priorities.
```

## Site monitor

Checks all live client sites every week.

- **Starts:** schedule, Mondays at 7:00
- **Reads:** List of live sites
- **Tools:** Web browser (read)
- **Delivers:** Problems only
- **You approve:** None needed; read only.
- **Saves:** 0.2 hours a week

```text
Check every live client site for broken links, SSL certificates expiring within 30 days, and mobile speed below 80. Report only problems, one line each with the site and what to fix. If all is fine, stay silent.
```

## Launch checker

Runs the launch checklist on a client site before it goes live.

- **Starts:** on request, Before each launch
- **Reads:** Site address
- **Tools:** Web browser (read), Speed test (read)
- **Needs:** Launch checklist
- **Delivers:** Fix list and client summary
- **You approve:** A person fixes issues and sends the summary.
- **Saves:** 0.1 to 0.2 hours a week

```text
Open the site and check: speed scores above 90 on mobile and desktop, alt texts present and in German, no broken links, meta titles and descriptions, favicon, 404 page, redirects from old URLs, cookie banner, Search Console access. List the fixes, most important first, with the page for each. Then draft a five-line summary for the client.
```

## Inquiry responder

Drafts replies to new project inquiries.

- **Starts:** new item, A new mail arrives in the contact inbox
- **Reads:** Contact inbox
- **Tools:** Contact mailbox (draft)
- **Needs:** Inquiry replies
- **Delivers:** A reply draft
- **You approve:** A person sends the reply.
- **Saves:** 0.1 hours a week

```text
If the mail is a new project inquiry, draft a reply: thank them, repeat what they asked for in one line, two lines on how the studio works, and the booking link for a 30-minute call. German unless they wrote in English. Post a one-line summary with what they want and any budget. Skip newsletters and notifications.
```
